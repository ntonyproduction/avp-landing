How it works

From a phrase to your timeline.

  1. Type your phrase.

    Write it the way it is said, "the algorithm" rather than "algorithm": every page uses it mid-sentence. RESHUFFLE deals a new set of pages.

  2. Set the look while it plays.

    The preview loops with its sound, so you see and hear the cut before you render. It shows the clip's length too, for cutting to a beat.

  3. Press RENDER.

    In Resolve Studio the clip lands at the playhead, above your footage, sound and all. In Premiere Pro, drag it from the app onto your timeline.

The whole app · one panel

What you can change.

Paperboy is a small app that sits beside your editor. Every control is on its front, and the preview shows each change as you make it. The defaults already look the part.

Keyword
The phrase highlighted on every page. RESHUFFLE deals new pages, and a right-click goes back to the last deal.
Last card
The headline the clip ends on, with your phrase in it. The cuts are too fast to read; this page holds.
Cards
How many pages. 24 unless you change it.
Speed
How fast it cuts. The readout gives the cuts per second at your timeline's frame rate.
Word
How big the phrase stands in the frame.
Rhythm
Steady gives every page the same length. Ramp starts slow and speeds up into the last page.
Style and paper
A newspaper page or plain lines of big text, on light paper or a charcoal sheet.
Blur
How far the page blurs away from the phrase, up and down or all round. The phrase stays sharp.
Chromatic
Colour fringes growing out from the phrase, the way a lens makes them.
Highlight
The marker's colour, from the classic yellow to any hex code, a brand's included.
Sound
Five paper sounds, one on every cut, carried in the clip. Off until you pick one.
To Resolve playhead
Lit when Resolve Studio is answering: RENDER then puts the clip straight onto your timeline.

Set once, in the cog

The clip's size and frame rate when Resolve is not setting them: vertical out of the box, or 16:9, square, 4:5, 4K or a size you type. Where the phrase sits in the frame, so it stays clear of the Shorts and Reels captions. How long the last page holds. And a little camera life: jitter, drift, push-in and softness.
