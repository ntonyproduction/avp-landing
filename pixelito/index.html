· seven sliders

What you can change.

In DaVinci Resolve everything is on the clip, in the Inspector. The defaults already look the part.

Pixel Size
How big the pixels are. It scales with the footage, so HD and 4K look the same.
Colour Depth
How many shades of each colour survive. Lower is cruder.
Dither
The fine checker pattern the old consoles drew to hide colour banding.
Frame Rate
Pictures per second. 12 gives the choppy motion of an old game.
Jitter
The resolution itself jumps a little, a few times a second.
Wobble
Patches of pixels shift about, like wobbling 3D polygons. Off until you want it.
Softness
Softens the picture sideways, like an old TV cable.

In Premiere Pro: four presets

One for each sequence shape: 16:9, 9:16, square and 4:5. Each is Premiere's own Mosaic, Posterize and Posterize Time, set up for you, and goes on an adjustment layer above your clips. Premiere has no dither and no random numbers, so the checker pattern and the jumping grid are in the Resolve version only.
