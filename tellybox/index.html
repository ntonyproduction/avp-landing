· Perfect for documentaries

Make your screen recordings cinematic.

Documentaries keep needing the same shot: a page, a map or a message on a screen, seen over someone's shoulder.

Tellybox gives you that shot from the recording itself, with the camera exactly where you want it.

Any recording with words
Finance, music software, a web page: if the text is the point, Tellybox puts a camera on it.
Switch the screen
LCD, CRT or a phone's OLED from one menu, and a preset to start a look from.
Move one slider, change the shot
Tilt, Lens Bulge, RGB Split, Moire: each one changes the shot as you move it.

Up close

Each part, on its own.

A few seconds each, rendered by Tellybox in DaVinci Resolve: the effect's own output, nothing added after.

Screen

Three screens, three kinds of pixel.

LCD for a laptop or monitor, CRT for an old TV, Phone for an OLED's tiny dots. The same recording on each, up close.

Phone, Push In, Drift

Vertical recordings, on a phone.

Set the screen to Phone and a vertical recording looks like a phone filmed up close. Tilt it, set Push In and Drift, and the camera moves on its own. On a 16:9 timeline, Recording Shape keeps the bars off the screen.

Lens Bulge

Curve the glass.

Lens Bulge rounds the screen out like an old TV's, or pulls it the other way. Here with Vignette and Chromatic Aberration darkening and fringing the edges.

Flicker, Flicker Speed

An old TV's flicker, up to a full glitch.

Flicker makes the screen's brightness waver. Flicker Speed runs from a slow breathing to a random glitch at the top. Flicker Glow Only holds the picture steady and pulses just the glow.

Focus Blur, Focus Shift

Soft edges, and a sharp band you steer.

Focus Blur sets how shallow the shot is: turn it up and the near and far sides of the screen fall away. Focus Shift slides the sharp band and Focus Angle turns it, and Focus Pull moves it over the clip with no keyframes.

Chromatic Aberration, RGB Split

Fringes, and an old TV's colour split.

Chromatic Aberration fringes the corners like a simple lens; RGB Split pulls red and blue apart. Here on a code editor, with RGB Split moving.

Moire

The bands a camera catches off a screen.

Moire adds the rippling bands you get filming a real monitor, stronger as the camera gets closer. Moire Bands, Moire Colour and Moire Speed set their size, tint and movement. Off by default.
