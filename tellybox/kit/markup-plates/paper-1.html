Small Models, Long Memory:
How Context Length Shapes Reasoning in Compact Language Models

Maya Okafor, Lukas Brandt, Sana Ito

Northfield Institute for Machine Learning

Abstract

We study how the length of the context window changes the multi-step reasoning of language models with fewer than eight billion parameters. Across 41,000 problems, a longer window helped more than a larger model, and doubling the parameters added only 6.1 points. Beyond 32k the gain flattens, and we find no benefit at 128k. We release the benchmark and every model's outputs.

Key result. Accuracy rose from 61.2% to 84.7% at 32k tokens.
