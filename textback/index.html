How it works

From a few messages to your timeline.

  1. Type the conversation.

    Type a message and press Enter: the next one is from the other person. SAMPLE puts in a chat to start from.

  2. Pick the look while it plays.

    The preview loops on the phone's screen, so every change shows as you make it. Its speaker key plays the sounds.

  3. Press RENDER.

    Drag the clip from the app onto your timeline, in any editor. In Resolve Studio it lands at the playhead by itself, above your footage, sound and all.

Type & Send, Header

Your side, typed out.

On the phone styles, Type & Send types your messages into the app's message bar, letter by letter, then sends them up. HEADER puts the contact on top.

Video, Voice, Photo

More than text.

Switch on PLAY and a video plays in its bubble or a voice note fills its waveform, and the chat waits for it. Photos, GIFs, reactions and read receipts too. They play silent: their sound is yours to add on the timeline.

+ Person, Group name

Group chats, up to six.

Add people and each gets a name and an avatar, the way the app shows them. Name the group and the header says it. An avatar can be a picture of your own.

Notification

A lock screen, not a chat.

The Notification style turns their messages into lock-screen notifications, the newest on top. Yours never show, as on a real phone.

Format

Any shape your timeline is.

Vertical out of the box, or 16:9, square, 4:5, 4K and a size you type. On a horizontal frame the chat scales up with it, so nothing needs resizing. In Resolve Studio, FORMAT follows the timeline by itself.
