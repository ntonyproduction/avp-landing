How it works

No keyframes. No After Effects.

A title is one clip on your timeline. Type in three boxes and the animation follows your words.

Drop it on
Resolve: Effects > Titles > Audio Vision Productions > Titulo. Premiere: Graphics Templates > My Templates, search "AVP Titulo".
Type three lines
A line on top, the main word and a line below. Then set the Scale, the main colour and the fonts.
Horizontal or vertical
On a 9:16 timeline Resolve sizes each title for it on its own. In Premiere, pick Vertical in the Frame menu.
Any length
Trim the clip longer or shorter: the title still lands on time and leaves at the clip's end.
