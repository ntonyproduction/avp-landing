How it works

From a link to your timeline.

  1. Paste the link, press LOAD.

    A waveform and a strip of frames appear in a few seconds. Nothing big downloads yet: Trawler reads only what it needs to draw them.

  2. Drag across the screen.

    Select the section, or type exact in and out times. Zoom in for the precise frame, and press Space to play the section first.

  3. Press HAUL.

    Only that section comes down. In Resolve Studio it lands at the playhead, picture and sound. Anywhere else, drag the file from the app into your edit.

The whole app · one panel

What you can change.

Trawler is a small app that sits beside your editor. Everything is on its front. The defaults already work: paste, drag, HAUL.

Link and LOAD
YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X, Reddit, SoundCloud and many more. Press the button again while it works and it stops within a second.
Waveform and film strip
Drag to select, scroll to zoom, and play the section before you take it. Type exact times if you know them.
AUDIO / VIDEO
Take the picture and sound, or only the sound. Video arrives as an H.264 MP4 that any editor opens, audio as WAV or MP3.
Format list
The quality, up to the best the link offers: 1080p as standard and 2160p where there is one.
An exact cut
The section starts where you put it, on the frame. A 15 second grab from a 10 minute video downloads about 5 MB, not the whole thing.
TO RESOLVE PLAYHEAD
A lamp that says where HAUL is sending the clip: to your playhead or just the Media Pool, as set in the cog. In Resolve Studio it puts the clip straight onto your timeline.
The boat
Shows how far the grab has got, with the time taken and, on the longer ones, how long is left.

Set once, in the cog

Save folder
Where the files go.
DaVinci Resolve
Whether HAUL sends the clip to Resolve, and whether it places it at the playhead.
Normalize audio
Raises a quiet sound until its loudest point sits just under zero, and tells you by how much. It never turns anything down, and it is off until you tick it.
Logins
When a link wants one (a private video, some Instagram posts), Trawler lists the browsers it can borrow your signed-in session from. On Windows that is Firefox.
Looks after itself
It checks for updates by itself and keeps its own folder tidy.
