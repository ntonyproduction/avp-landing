Three rules · read these first

Where it goes, and what it does not do.

✓On the voice track.

On the track the knob is instant, and one Velvox covers every clip on it.

  • Resolve: the Fairlight page, Mixer. On your voice track's strip, click + in the Effects slot and pick Velvox.
  • Premiere: Window > Audio Track Mixer. Show the effects (the small triangle at its top left), then pick Velvox in an empty effect slot on the voice track.
  • Final Cut: it has no tracks, so put Velvox on the voice clip, or on a compound clip holding the whole voice.

✕Not on the clip in Resolve. A clip effect is rendered ahead of time, so every knob move is heard a second or two late.

✓A quiet room.

You do not need a treated studio, just no wind, no traffic and no fan.

✕It does not remove noise. When it lifts a quiet take, the hiss comes up with the voice. Recorded somewhere noisy? Clean the clip first, then put Velvox on the track.

  • Resolve: Noise Reduction (or Voice Isolation in Resolve Studio), on the clip.
  • Premiere: Essential Sound > Reduce Noise or Enhance Speech, on the clip.
  • Final Cut: Voice Isolation or Noise Removal in the Audio inspector, on the clip.

✓Voice only on that track.

Velvox is made for a voice. Keep music on a track of its own.

✕Not with music. Music on the voice track swells in the pauses.

The whole plugin · one knob, one lamp

What you can change.

Amount runs from 0 to 100 and starts at 50. At 0, Velvox is close to off. From 25% up, your voice lands at upload level by itself, and the knob sets how smooth and clear it sounds.

Under the knob are nine stages, in this order. You never set them one by one.

  1. Rumble cutCuts the low rumble under the voice.
  2. De-mudA dip in the low mids, where a voice sounds boxy.
  3. LevelerRides your level, up to +30 dB on a quiet take.
  4. CompressorEvens out the words, and looks ahead so a phrase after a pause does not jump out.
  5. Presence and airBrings the voice forward and opens up the top.
  6. De-esserA light one, for sharp S sounds.
  7. Pause expanderBetween phrases it pulls the room back down, so lifting your voice does not lift the room as much.
  8. LoudnessLands the voice at about -16 LUFS, from 25% up.
  9. Safety limiterKeeps the peaks under -1 dBFS.

The lamp tells you when it is ready.

The meter reads your voice over the last 3 seconds of talking, in LUFS. Gold, ready to upload, is -18 to -14. Amber is quieter than that, red is louder.
